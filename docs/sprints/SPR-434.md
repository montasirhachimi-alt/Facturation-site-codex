# SPR-434 — Financial Statements & Finance Intelligence V1

Status: Completed
Date: 2026-08-17
Sprint type: Medium / Large Capability Sprint

## Executive Summary

SPR-434 turns canonical posted Accounting history into business-readable Finance statements:

- Profit & Loss / Compte de résultat
- Balance Sheet / Bilan
- Finance Overview statement metrics
- one limited Global Dashboard Finance contribution

The statements are derived read models. No financial statement tables, snapshots, stored balances or statutory localization layers were introduced.

## Repository Reconciliation

Existing Accounting foundations were sufficient:

- accounts have global `type` classification;
- accounts have normal balance metadata;
- journal entries and lines store debit/credit amounts;
- posted entries are protected;
- General Ledger and Trial Balance already implement date scopes and closing balances;
- monetary arithmetic uses integer minor-unit helpers.

No fiscal-year, period-closing, retained-earnings or localization foundation exists yet. SPR-434 therefore uses explicit date ranges and a visible current-period-result component.

## Account Classification

Financial statements reuse the global Accounting Core categories:

- `asset`
- `liability`
- `equity`
- `income`
- `expense`

No country-specific account class is hardcoded.

## Profit & Loss Formula

Profit & Loss is period-based:

```text
fromDate -> toDate
```

Only posted journal entry lines inside the selected period contribute.

Formula:

```text
Revenue = income account credits - income account debits
Expenses = expense account debits - expense account credits
Net Result = Revenue - Expenses
```

Rows remain account-level for traceability back to Ledger movements.

## Balance Sheet Formula

Balance Sheet is cumulative as of `asOfDate`.

Formula:

```text
Assets = asset account debit balance - credit balance
Liabilities = liability account credit balance - debit balance
Equity = equity account credit balance - debit balance
```

Because BOSIACO does not yet implement fiscal closing or retained earnings transfer, current period result remains a separate derived component:

```text
Assets = Liabilities + Equity + Current Period Result
```

The report exposes reconciliation difference instead of forcing totals to match.

## Period Semantics

SPR-434 does not create persisted fiscal periods.

Supported semantics:

- Profit & Loss: selected period movements.
- Balance Sheet: cumulative position as of selected date.
- Current Period Result: Profit & Loss result for the selected `fromDate -> asOfDate`.

Fiscal closing, retained earnings, opening entries, period locks and fiscal calendars are deferred.

## Source Of Truth

Statements derive from:

```text
POSTED AccountingJournalEntryLine
```

Draft entries are excluded. Sales commercial totals do not appear in Finance statements unless they have been posted into Accounting through SPR-433.

## Commercial Accounting Integration

SPR-433-generated Sales invoice/payment journal entries naturally flow into:

- Profit & Loss through revenue accounts;
- Balance Sheet through receivable and settlement accounts.

There is no Sales-specific statement shortcut.

## KPI Semantics

| KPI | Source | Formula | Period | Currency | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chiffre d'affaires | Profit & Loss | income credits - income debits | selected/current period | functional currency | only posted Accounting entries |
| Charges | Profit & Loss | expense debits - expense credits | selected/current period | functional currency | no AP/inventory valuation unless posted manually |
| Résultat net | Profit & Loss | revenue - expenses | selected/current period | functional currency | no retained earnings transfer |
| Équilibre bilan | Balance Sheet | assets vs liabilities + equity + current result | as of selected/current date | functional currency | management statement, not statutory localized report |
