# SPR-439 — Supplier Payment / AP Settlement V1

## Summary

SPR-439 introduces the canonical Supplier Payment / AP Settlement V1 flow.

The Procurement lifecycle now supports:

```text
Supplier Bill
  -> AP liability posting
  -> Supplier Payment
  -> AP settlement posting
```

Supplier Payments are Procurement-owned business records linked to Supplier Bills. Finance posts finalized Supplier Payments as source-linked Accounting entries.

## Root Cause

Before SPR-439, BOSIACO could post Supplier Bills to Accounts Payable but had no canonical settlement object linked to those bills.

The legacy `CashEntry` model could not safely represent this workflow because it is not linked to Procurement Supplier Bills and does not preserve AP source traceability. Reusing `SalesPayment` would mix customer receivable settlement with supplier payable settlement.

## Data Model

Added `ProcurementSupplierPayment`.

Key fields:

- `tenantCompanyId`
- `workspaceId`
- `number`
- `supplierId`
- `supplierName`
- `supplierBillId`
- `supplierBillNumber`
- `paymentDate`
- `amount`
- `currency`
- `method`
- `reference`
- `notes`
- `status`
- `finalizedAt`
- `accountedAt`
- lifecycle timestamps

Each Supplier Payment belongs to one Supplier Bill in V1. One Supplier Bill may receive multiple Supplier Payments until its outstanding balance reaches zero.

## Business Rules

Supported in V1:

- one Supplier Payment settles one Supplier Bill;
- one Supplier Bill may receive multiple Supplier Payments;
- partial payments are supported;
- subsequent payments settle the remaining balance;
- cumulative payments cannot exceed the Supplier Bill total outstanding amount;
- amount must be greater than zero;
- payment currency must match the Supplier Bill currency;
- Supplier Bill must be accounted before payment recording;
- Supplier Payment must be finalized before Accounting posting;
- accounted Supplier Payments cannot be silently modified through Procurement persistence.

Deferred:

- one payment allocated across multiple Supplier Bills;
- advance payments;
- bank reconciliation;
- remittance advice;
- controlled reposting after reversal;
- FX differences.

## Accounting

Supplier Payment posting creates:

```text
Dr Accounts Payable
Cr Settlement Account
```

The Accounting source is:

```text
sourceType = procurement.supplier-payment
sourceId = ProcurementSupplierPayment.id
```

Posting is idempotent through the existing Accounting source uniqueness model.

Supplier Payment posting does not touch expense, tax, stock, GRNI or COGS accounts.

## Procurement UI

`Factures fournisseurs` now shows:

- payment status;
- paid amount;
- outstanding amount;
- total TTC;
- `Enregistrer un règlement` action for accounted bills with a remaining balance.

The payment dialog records:

- payment date;
- amount;
- method;
- reference;
- notes.

## Finance UI

`Intégration achats` now has two AP source sections:

- Supplier Bills to post;
- Supplier Payments to post.

The AP settlement account is marked as required for supplier payment posting.

## Persistence

Supplier Payment persistence is tenant-scoped and uses the existing Procurement persistence route.

Procurement local state remains a client cache. Database persistence remains authoritative.

## Validation

Implemented runtime validations for:

- Supplier Payment model and migration presence;
- Procurement UI and Finance UI exposure;
- deterministic AP settlement journal entry mapping;
- required settlement account;
- draft payment rejection;
- cumulative outstanding protection;
- Accounting source idempotency wiring.

## Known Limitations

- V1 supports multiple Supplier Payments for the same Supplier Bill, but each Supplier Payment belongs to exactly one Supplier Bill.
- One payment allocated across multiple Supplier Bills is deferred.
- Supplier payment posting still requires configured AP posting settings and an open accounting period.
- Controlled reposting after reversal is still deferred.
- Bank reconciliation and cash/bank statement matching are not implemented.
