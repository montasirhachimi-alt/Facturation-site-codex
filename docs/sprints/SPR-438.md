# SPR-438 — Goods Receipt ↔ Supplier Bill ↔ GRNI Reconciliation V1

## Summary

SPR-438 closes the inbound inventory accounting gap between Procurement, Inventory and Finance.

BOSIACO can now account for stocked purchases without double-recognizing cost:

```text
Goods Receipt valuation
  -> Dr Inventory Asset
  -> Cr GRNI

Supplier Bill for stocked receipt
  -> Dr GRNI
  -> Dr Recoverable Tax when configured
  -> Cr Accounts Payable
```

Non-stock Supplier Bills continue to use the SPR-436 expense/AP formula.

## Repository Reconciliation

Goods Receipt lines already map deterministically to Purchase Order lines through `purchaseOrderLineId`.

Supplier Bill lines already carry optional `purchaseOrderLineId`, `goodsReceiptLineId` and `productId`, which is sufficient for a narrow V1 reconciliation without creating a full matching engine.

Inventory valuation from SPR-437 already creates durable inbound `InventoryValuationEvent` records from posted stock movements. Those events are the accounting source for receipt capitalization.

## GRNI Architecture

GRNI configuration is Finance-owned through `AccountingInventoryPostingSettings.grniClearingAccountId`.

Accounting source identities:

- receipt capitalization: `sourceType = inventory.receipt-valuation`, `sourceId = InventoryValuationEvent.id`;
- supplier bill AP/GRNI clearing: `sourceType = procurement.supplier-bill`, `sourceId = ProcurementSupplierBill.id`;
- outbound COGS remains `sourceType = inventory.cogs`.

## Goods Receipt Accounting

Finance can post a valued inbound receipt event as:

```text
Dr Inventory Asset
Cr GRNI
```

The amount comes from the durable `InventoryValuationEvent.totalValue`. Accounting does not recalculate historical cost from mutable Product price or current Purchase Order data.

## Supplier Bill Accounting Changes

Supplier Bill posting is now conditional:

- stock-backed linked receipt lines debit GRNI;
- non-stock or unlinked expense lines debit the configured Purchase/Expense account;
- recoverable tax is debited separately when tax exists;
- Accounts Payable is credited for the Supplier Bill total.

This prevents stocked purchases from hitting P&L merely because the supplier invoice arrived.

## Stock vs Expense Classification

V1 classifies a Supplier Bill line as stock-backed only when all are true:

- the line references a Goods Receipt line;
- the line references the same Product as the receipt line;
- the Product is stock-tracked;
- an inbound `InventoryValuationEvent` exists for the receipt line movement;
- the receipt valuation has already been posted to GRNI.

All other lines remain expense/non-stock lines.

## Matching Model

No broad matching framework was introduced.

The V1 match is computed at posting time from existing durable links:

- Supplier Bill line;
- Goods Receipt line;
- Product;
- inbound valuation event;
- receipt capitalization journal entry.

Traceability is preserved through source-linked Accounting entries.

## Partial Matching

Partial Supplier Bills are supported when a bill line quantity does not exceed the referenced received quantity and the tax-exclusive bill amount matches the proportional receipt valuation.

Receipt value greater than bill value can clear GRNI partially through the bill quantity.

Bill quantity greater than received quantity is rejected.

## Price Variance

Price variance is detected by comparing:

- tax-exclusive Supplier Bill line amount after line/global discount;
- proportional inbound valuation amount.

If the variance is 0.01 or more, posting is rejected. V1 does not rewrite inventory valuation and does not post price variance entries.

## Tax Treatment

GRNI matching uses tax-exclusive values.

Recoverable tax continues to post separately through the configured AP tax recoverable account.

No country-specific VAT localization was added.

## Period Control

Receipt capitalization and Supplier Bill GRNI clearing both use canonical Accounting period controls. Posting into closed periods is rejected server-side.

## Reversal Behavior

Receipt capitalization and Supplier Bill entries are normal posted `AccountingJournalEntry` records and remain compatible with the SPR-435 reversal workflow.

Reversal does not mutate physical stock, valuation events, goods receipts or supplier bills.

## UI Changes

Finance `Intégration stock` now includes:

- GRNI account mapping;
- valued inbound receipt queue;
- controlled receipt capitalization action;
- existing COGS queue.

Finance `Intégration achats` now displays whether a Supplier Bill is treated as `Stock / GRNI` or `Charges`.

Procurement workflows were not redesigned.

## Migration

Created:

- `prisma/migrations/20260818133000_grni_reconciliation_v1/migration.sql`

The migration adds `grniClearingAccountId` to `AccountingInventoryPostingSettings`.

Migration was created and validated locally. It was not applied to a remote database in this sprint.

## Validation

Runtime validation now covers:

- GRNI clearing for stocked Supplier Bills without double expense;
- inbound receipt capitalization `Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI`;
- active Finance UI exposure for receipt capitalization;
- prior receipt capitalization requirement before Supplier Bill GRNI clearing;
- existing COGS regression;
- SPR-437A short write transaction guard.

## Deferred Scope

Deferred intentionally:

- supplier payments;
- AP aging;
- bank reconciliation;
- landed costs;
- full price variance accounting;
- automated background posting;
- full matching persistence;
- advanced GRNI reporting;
- statutory VAT localization;
- FX conversion;
- FIFO/LIFO;
- manufacturing costing.

## Recommended Next Capability

The next natural capability is Supplier Payment / AP Settlement, so Procurement can move from Supplier Bill liability to controlled payment and payable clearing.
