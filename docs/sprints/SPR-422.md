# SPR-422 — Products & Inventory Operational Workspace

## Summary

SPR-422 turns the existing Product Catalog and Inventory foundations into visible operational workspaces.

The sprint keeps the platform-driven activation model: Products and Inventory are exposed through module descriptors and the current Alpha Edition activation, not through hard-coded sidebar entries.

## Delivered Product Outcome

- Products are visible from navigation as `Stock → Produits`.
- Inventory is visible from navigation as `Stock → Stock`.
- Product list displays persisted Product data with operational stock columns.
- Product create/edit supports the persisted reorder point.
- Product detail page is available at `/sales/products/[productId]`.
- Product detail shows Product information, stock overview and recent Inventory movements.
- Inventory workspace remains backed by the canonical Inventory engine and persisted snapshots.

## Product List

The Product list now shows:

- SKU
- product name
- category
- sales price
- purchase price
- on-hand quantity
- reserved quantity
- available quantity
- reorder point
- stock status
- active/archive status

Search, category filter, unit filter, lifecycle status filter, stock-status filter, sorting and pagination operate on the current Product and Inventory snapshots.

## Product Creation And Editing

Product creation and editing reuse the existing Product service and persistence bridge.

The dialog now includes:

- product identity
- SKU
- category
- unit
- purchase price
- selling price
- VAT
- stockable/service classification
- reorder point

The reorder point accepts the same comma/dot decimal input policy used by Inventory quantity helpers and is persisted as `Product.reorderPoint`.

## Product Details

The new Product detail page contains:

- Product Information
- Stock Overview
- Recent Inventory Movements

The page does not query Prisma directly. It consumes the Product Catalog and Inventory client snapshots hydrated through the existing persistence providers.

## Stock Status Policy

Stock status is derived from canonical quantities:

- `Inactive`
- `Out of Stock`
- `Low Stock`
- `Reserved`
- `In Stock`

Quantities are summarized from Inventory balances and normalized through Inventory helpers.

## Activation

`sales.products` and `inventory.stock` are now Alpha-ready and active in the current `alpha.crm-sales` Edition profile.

Still inactive in Alpha:

- Sales Orders
- Delivery Notes
- Procurement
- Purchasing
- HR
- AI

## Architecture

New shared Product stock summary helper:

```text
Product + InventoryBalance[] + StockMovement[]
        ↓
ProductStockSummary
        ↓
Product list / Product detail
```

The UI consumes the prepared summary and does not duplicate Inventory posting rules.
