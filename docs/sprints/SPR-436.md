# SPR-436 — Procurement / AP Accounting V1

## Summary

SPR-436 connects Procurement to Finance through a controlled Accounts Payable foundation.

Procurement remains the operational source for suppliers, purchase orders and goods receipts. Finance remains the financial source for journal entries, ledgers and reports. Supplier Bills now bridge both domains.

## Repository Reconciliation

The existing `PurchaseInvoice` Prisma model was audited and left untouched.

It is not used as the canonical AP model because it belongs to the legacy purchasing path:

- relation to legacy `Supplier`;
- relation to legacy `Product`;
- no link to `ProcurementSupplier`;
- no link to operational Purchase Orders or Goods Receipts;
- no source-idempotent Accounting integration.

SPR-436 introduces `ProcurementSupplierBill` as the active Procurement/AP source.

## Supplier Bill Model

Added durable Procurement Supplier Bills with:

- supplier;
- supplier reference;
- bill date;
- due date;
- currency;
- optional Purchase Order link;
- optional Goods Receipt link;
- line items;
- discount rate;
- tax rate per line;
- notes;
- lifecycle status;
- tenant/workspace scoping.

Line items preserve Product identity where selected.

## Lifecycle

Supported V1 statuses:

- `draft`;
- `finalized`;
- `accounted`;
- `cancelled`;
- `archived`.

Draft bills are editable. Finalized bills can be posted to Accounting. Accounted bills are protected against silent mutation.

## Accounting Configuration

Added tenant-scoped AP posting settings:

- Purchase Journal;
- Accounts Payable account;
- Expense/Purchase account;
- Settlement/Bank account, prepared but not used by supplier payment V1;
- Recoverable Tax account;
- Functional currency.

## Posting Formula

Supplier Bill posting:

- Debit Expense/Purchase account for net HT amount after discount;
- Debit Recoverable Tax account when tax exists;
- Credit Accounts Payable for total TTC.

The entry is balanced, posted and traceable with:

- `sourceType = procurement.supplier-bill`;
- `sourceId = SupplierBill.id`.

## Idempotency

The existing Accounting unique source constraint is reused:

`tenantCompanyId + sourceType + sourceId`

The same Supplier Bill cannot create duplicate live journal entries.

## Period Control

AP posting uses the existing SPR-435 period-control rule.

If the bill date belongs to a closed Accounting period, posting is rejected server-side.

## Supplier Payment Decision

Supplier Payment/AP settlement is explicitly deferred.

Reason: the only existing payment-like model, `CashEntry`, is legacy/demo-era and not linked to active `ProcurementSupplierBill` records. Reusing it would create a weak AP settlement workflow.

## UI

Procurement adds:

- `/procurement/supplier-bills`;
- Supplier Bill list;
- search;
- status filter;
- create/edit draft Supplier Bill dialog;
- finalization action;
- navigation entry under Achats.

Finance adds:

- `Intégration achats` tab;
- AP posting settings;
- Supplier Bills ready to post;
- controlled Supplier Bill posting button;
- visible notice that supplier payments are deferred.
