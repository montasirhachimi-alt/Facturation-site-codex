# SPR-427 — Sales Operations Profile Activation & Release Hardening

Status: Completed
Date: 2026-08-17

## Executive Summary

SPR-427 promotes the validated Sales Operations workflow into the default `alpha.crm-sales` product profile.

The promotion is activation-only. It uses the existing Edition Profile, Module Registry, Activation Engine, dynamic navigation, route availability, dashboard contribution and Command Center architecture.

No Runtime, Prisma, persistence, permissions, authentication, Inventory posting, Delivery Note posting, Shipment lifecycle or business workflow behavior changed.

## Pre-Activation State

Before this promotion:

- `sales.orders` was registered, persisted, routed and `alphaReady`, but not listed in `alpha.crm-sales`.
- `sales.delivery-notes` was registered, persisted, routed and `alphaReady`, but not listed in `alpha.crm-sales`.
- `sales.shipments` was registered, persisted, routed and `alphaReady`, but not listed in `alpha.crm-sales`.
- the internal `sales-operations` profile activated the three modules for controlled QA.
- route availability, sidebar navigation, dashboard contributions, record search and Command Center visibility were already activation-driven.

The exact gate was the `enabledModuleIds` list in `src/platform/editions/edition.profiles.ts`.

## Manual QA Evidence

Authenticated end-to-end Sales Operations QA validated:

```text
Quote
→ Quote acceptance
→ Sales Order
→ Stock reservation
→ Partial Delivery Note
→ Delivery Note posting
→ Reservation consumption
→ Inventory ISSUE
→ Shipment creation
→ Shipment lifecycle
→ Remaining Delivery Note
→ Final Delivery Note posting
→ Complete Sales Order delivery
→ Final inventory reconciliation
```

Validated quantity scenario:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Initial stock | 200 | 0 | 200 | 0 |
| After reservation | 200 | 8 | 192 | 0 |
| After first delivery of 3 | 197 | 5 | 192 | 3 |
| After final delivery of 5 | 192 | 0 | 192 | 8 |

Final Sales Order state:

- ordered: 8;

- reserved: 0;
- status: delivered / `Livrée`;
- remaining to reserve: 0;
- reservation state: non applicable.

## Audit Findings

### Sales Orders

- state: persisted and operational;
- gate: Edition activation profile;
- route: `/sales/orders` and `/sales/orders/[orderId]`;
- dependencies: CRM Companies, Product Catalog, persistence; optional CRM Contacts, Quotes and Inventory.

Sales Orders reserve stock through reservation movements only. They do not post Inventory `ISSUE` movements.

### Delivery Notes

- state: persisted and operational;
- gate: Edition activation profile;
- route: `/sales/delivery-notes` and `/sales/delivery-notes/[deliveryNoteId]`;
- dependencies: Sales Orders, Product Catalog, Inventory, CRM Companies and persistence.

Delivery Notes remain the physical stock owner for customer delivery. Posting creates Inventory `ISSUE` movements, consumes reservations and updates delivered Sales Order quantities transactionally.

### Shipments

- state: persisted and operational;
- gate: Edition activation profile;
- route: `/sales/shipments` and `/sales/shipments/[shipmentId]`;
- dependencies: Delivery Notes, Sales Orders and CRM Companies.

Shipments are logistics-only. Shipment lifecycle transitions do not mutate Inventory balances or movements.

## Activation Decision

Decision: **ACTIVATED**.

Sales Operations passed authenticated end-to-end QA and no remaining technical or product blocker was found in the activation audit.

The following modules are now enabled in `alpha.crm-sales`:

- `sales.orders`;
- `sales.delivery-notes`;
- `sales.shipments`.

The internal `sales-operations` profile remains available for development/test QA compatibility.

## Regression Protection

Preserved by runtime validation:

- Sales Order reservation uses `RESERVATION` / `RELEASE`, not `ISSUE`;
- Delivery Note posting owns Inventory `ISSUE`;
- partial delivery remains supported;
- complete delivery remains supported;
- reservation consumption stays inside Delivery Note posting;
- Shipment lifecycle remains persisted and logistics-only;
- Shipment repository does not import Inventory modules;
- SPR-425A calculation remains:

```text
remainingToReserve = max(ordered - delivered - currentlyReserved, 0)
```
