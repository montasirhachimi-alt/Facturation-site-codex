# SPR-429 — Post-Procurement Roadmap Reconciliation & Next Core Domain Decision

Status: Completed
Date: 2026-08-17

## Executive Summary

SPR-429 is a documentation and product-decision sprint. No new ERP domain was implemented.

The repository now shows that BOSIACO Alpha has moved beyond the earlier CRM/Sales prototype stage. The current default `alpha.crm-sales` profile activates CRM, Sales, Product Catalog, Inventory, Procurement and Sales Operations. Procurement is operational after SPR-423 through SPR-428, including Suppliers, Purchase Orders, Goods Receipts, partial receipt handling, Inventory `RECEIPT` posting and a read-only Procurement cockpit.

The next major direction should be **Accounting Foundation**.

This is the largest remaining ERP Core gap. Sales invoices and payments are commercial records, not accounting transactions. Procurement has supplier orders and goods receipts, but no supplier invoices, payable lifecycle, journal entries, chart of accounts, ledger, reconciliation, fiscal periods, VAT reporting or accounting posting engine.

## Accounting / Finance Existing State

The repository contains legacy financial-looking models and routes such as `Payment`, `PurchaseInvoice`, `PurchaseInvoiceLine`, `CashEntry`, `/caisse` and `/statistiques`, plus planned module descriptors `finance.cash` and `finance.reports`.

These are not a production Accounting module.

Confirmed missing Accounting foundations:

- Chart of accounts.
- Fiscal periods.
- Journal entries.
- General ledger.
- Trial balance.
- Profit and loss.
- Balance sheet.
- VAT declaration/reporting.
- Receivables subledger.
- Payables subledger.
- Supplier invoice operational workflow.
- Customer invoice accounting posting.
- Payment allocation/reconciliation.
- Bank/cash reconciliation.
- Accounting posting rules from Sales, Procurement and Inventory.

Current Sales invoices and payments should remain classified as commercial documents and collection records until a dedicated accounting engine posts them into a ledger.

## Sales Cockpit Assessment

Sales now has enough operational data to justify a future dedicated `Ventes -> Vue d'ensemble` cockpit:

- Quotes.
- Accepted quote conversion.
- Sales Orders.
- Reservations.
- Delivery Notes.
- Shipments.
- Invoices.
- Payments.

However, adding a Sales cockpit immediately after Procurement would be product-experience enrichment, not the next missing ERP Core capability. The global Dashboard already exposes Sales-oriented contributions, and Sales Operations is now visible in Alpha.

Recommendation: defer a dedicated Sales cockpit until after Accounting foundations clarify revenue, receivables, payment and posting semantics. Do not create a cockpit only for visual symmetry with Procurement.

## Inventory / Product Architecture Assessment

Product Catalog and Inventory are already separated correctly:

- `sales.products` owns the Product master data.
- `inventory.stock` owns warehouses, balances, movements, reservations and availability.
- Stock navigation groups Product Catalog and Inventory under one operational area.
- `/inventory` already functions as the effective Stock domain cockpit.

Genuine gaps remain, but they are depth items rather than the next core-domain decision:

- Lots and serial numbers.
- Barcode scanning.
- Advanced warehouse workflows.
- Stock valuation.
- Returns and reversals.
- Manufacturing and POS integration.
- Reorder automation.

These should not be rebuilt as new overview pages.

The platform is mature enough to add the next major ERP domain through existing contracts.

- Module Registry: `src/platform/modules/`.

- Dynamic Navigation and Route Availability: module-driven route ownership and middleware.
- Dashboard Contributions: `src/platform/dashboard/`.
- Command Center and Unified Search: provider/registry driven.
- Persistence: Prisma models plus tenant-scoped repositories and API routes.

None of these blocks beginning Accounting Foundation as a new module family.

## Recommended Next Major Direction

Recommended direction: **Accounting Foundation**.

Evidence:

- The current Alpha already covers the physical and commercial operational chain:
  `Company -> Contact -> Quote -> Sales Order -> Reservation -> Delivery Note -> Shipment -> Invoice -> Payment`.
- Procurement already covers:
  `Supplier -> Purchase Order -> Goods Receipt -> Inventory RECEIPT`.
- The largest remaining ERP Core break is financial truth:
  commercial documents exist, but no ledger or accounting posting exists.
- The Blueprint explicitly treats BOSIACO as a Business Operating System, not a set of decorative CRUD pages. Accounting Foundation advances the operational system more than another cockpit page.

## Proposed Objective For The Sprint After SPR-429

Create an **Accounting Foundation V1**.

Proposed scope:

- Define the Accounting module descriptors and activation status.
- Introduce a minimal chart of accounts foundation.
- Introduce fiscal period metadata if required by the current business rules.
- Introduce journal and journal entry foundations.
- Define an accounting posting interface that Sales, Procurement and Inventory can later call.
- Classify Sales invoices/payments and future supplier invoices as source documents, not ledger entries.
- Keep all posting deterministic, tenant-scoped and service-owned.
- Avoid full tax declaration, reconciliation automation or statutory reporting in V1.

This should start as a foundation/domain sprint, not as a visual cockpit sprint.
